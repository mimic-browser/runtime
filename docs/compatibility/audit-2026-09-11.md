# Chrome 152 differential coverage and baseline limitations

This reference describes revision `3faacc6`, not current compatibility.
Measured executable: `.build/mimic-audit.exe`, SHA256
`A80E2F47120B3B82C79C363F904707D5FE3DF1146E4304CBEE598EB63C86EEDC`.
Reference: Chrome **152.0.7977.82**, Windows, fresh headful profile, with
additional headless controls of the same version.

Probes ran on localhost in separate processes/Contexts. They do not measure
remote TLS/JA4 or explain an external service's verdict. Current capabilities
belong in [the compatibility reference](../compatibility.md) and the semantic
CDP support registry.

## Inconclusive and excluded observations

- cross_realm_reflection aborts while normalizing results in both browsers, with
  different errors. It does not establish a particular reflection defect.
- Four attempts lack #parsed-template and are excluded. The 119 connection
  failures after the native crash are not 119 further defects.
- downlink 1.45 versus 10 reflects environment/estimation. Exact resource
  durations and headful/headless user agents are not fixed-value expectations.
- Stack labels anonymous/eval/__pyppeteer_evaluation_script__ require equal CDP
  source attribution before comparing exception semantics.
- A01 occurred during background snapshot creation. The timing/mark probe's
  name does not establish Performance.mark as its cause.

## Separate architectural limits

These were code/documentation boundaries, **not new corpus measurements**:
whole-body buffering before Fetch streaming; HTTP/3 dynamic QPACK and unmeasured
packet timing/congestion; cross-realm define/delete/newTarget and retained
callbacks; cross-document history/BFCache/events; document.write with
async/defer/modules and policy containers/client hints; graphics readback
consistency, fonts, SVG ink, image orientation/color/srcset, WebGPU execution
and buffer limits.

References: [network profile](chrome152-network-profile-2026-09-11.md),
[realm ownership](../html-all-collection.md), [architecture](../architecture.md)
and [compatibility](../compatibility.md). Earlier bootstrap/Wasm timings,
resolved document.all cases and general network claims are not automatically
evidence for this measured baseline.
